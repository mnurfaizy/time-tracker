Privacy Policy

Time Tracker · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Time Tracker is an internal time tracking application used by our team to record working hours on clients and projects. This page explains what data the application stores, how Google user data is used, and how you can remove it.

Data we store

  • Account data: your name, email address, role and a securely hashed password.
  • Time tracking data: time entries you create (description, start and end time, project, task, tags, billable flag), plus the clients, projects and tags of the workspace.
  • Session data: a login cookie used only to keep you signed in.

Google user data

Connecting Google is optional. When you choose Continue with Google or Connect Google Calendar, the application requests:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile) — only to identify which existing account you are signing in to.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events) — to show the events of your primary calendar on the Calendar page so you can turn them into time entries, and, only if you switch it on, to create and update events in your calendar for your own time entries.

Google Calendar events are read on demand and are not stored by the application. The only Google data kept is your Google email address and an access token that lets the application act on your behalf for the features above. Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in a managed database (Turso) and served by an application host (Render). Both are used only to run this application. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

Who can see your data

You can see your own time entries. Workspace administrators can see and manage the time entries of all members for reporting and billing. Depending on workspace settings, other members may see a team summary on the Dashboard.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Google at any time in Profile settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect. This revokes the access token and deletes it from the application. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your account or time entries deleted, contact your workspace administrator.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please contact the administrator of your Time Tracker workspace.
